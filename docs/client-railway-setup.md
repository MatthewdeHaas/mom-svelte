# Railway Setup Guide

This takes about 5 minutes. You only need to do this once.

## Step 1: Create an account

1. Go to https://railway.com
2. Click ***Sign In*** on the top right corner
3. Click the small button beneath the pop-up that says ***Log in using email***
4. Enter your email
5. Verify your email by clicking the email sent by Railway

## Step 2: Subscribe to the Hobby plan

1. Once logged in, go to **Settings** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Billing**
3. Subscribe to the **Hobby plan** ($5/month)
4. Enter your card details

The Hobby plan includes $5 of free usage each month, so for a small app like this one the hosting is effectively covered by the subscription itself.

## Step 3: Accept the project invite

1. Check your email for an invite from Railway to join the project from the developer (Matthew)
2. Click **Accept**

That's it. Once you've accepted, I'll transfer the project to your account so billing goes directly to you. You won't need to configure or manage anything technical.
